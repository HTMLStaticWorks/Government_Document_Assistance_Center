Our Mission: Frictionless Government Services.

We started with one goal: To help 10,000 families reclaim their time by simplifying government document processing once and for all.

Born From Bureaucratic Struggle

Our founders, Marcus and Sarah Jenkins, spent months navigating endless queues and confusing government paperwork just to renew a passport. After mastering the system for efficient document processing, they dedicated their lives to helping others do the same.

DocAssist isn't just a platform; it's a movement toward frictionless bureaucracy. We believe that when applications are handled effortlessly, you can focus on what truly matters in life.
