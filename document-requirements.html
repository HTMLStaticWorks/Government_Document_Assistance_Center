Required Documents Checklist

Come fully prepared for your appointment. Below are the mandatory documents you need to bring for each service.

Passport Assistance

Ensure you have original and self-attested photocopies of the following:

  • Proof of Address: Aadhaar Card, Utility Bill (Electricity/Water), or Bank Statement.
  • Proof of Date of Birth: Birth Certificate, 10th Standard Marksheet, or PAN Card.
  • Photographs: 2 recent passport-size photos with a white background.
  • Old Passport: (If applying for renewal) Original old passport and photocopy of the first and last page.

Driving License Services

For Learner's or Permanent License applications:

  • Age Proof: Birth Certificate, PAN Card, or 10th Certificate.
  • Address Proof: Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID.
  • Medical Certificate: Form 1A signed by a registered medical practitioner (required for certain categories/ages).
  • Learner's License: Original Learner's License (if applying for Permanent).

Aadhaar & PAN Services

For new applications or corrections:

  • Identity Proof: Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License.
  • Address Proof: Post Office passbook, Property Registration Document, or Utility Bill.
  • Date of Birth Proof: Birth Certificate, Matriculation Certificate, or Passport.
