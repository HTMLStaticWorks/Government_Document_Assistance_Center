Processing Questions & Guidance

Everything you need to know about processing timelines, fee transparency, privacy compliance, and our 100% money-back guarantee.

Is DocAssist an official government agency?

No. DocAssist is an independent document management consultancy. We streamline complex government administrative procedures, perform pre-verification, manage appointment slots, and expedite file preparation. Official government fees are paid directly to issuing agencies.

Independent Consultancy Pre-Validation Guaranteed

Are there any hidden charges or extra fees?

Never. Our service fee packages are 100% transparent and all-inclusive of our guidance, document review, and tracking portal access. Any required official government fees are itemized clearly upfront before you authorize payment.

Zero Hidden Charges Itemized Invoices

What is your 100% Money-Back Guarantee policy?

If our senior specialists review your application during pre-validation and determine that you do not meet government eligibility requirements before official submission, we will immediately issue a 100% full refund of our service fee.

100% Full Refundable Zero-Risk Guarantee

How long does expedited processing take?

While official government processing timelines depend on statutory department workloads, our automated file formatting and instant slot booking reduce overall processing wait times by up to 80%, placing your file in the fastest queue.

Up to 80% Faster Queue Synchronized Slot Booking

How is my sensitive personal document data secured?

All documents uploaded to our platform are protected by bank-level AES 256-bit encryption during upload and storage. Files are kept strictly in your private encrypted vault and automatically purged after official processing is complete.

AES 256-Bit Encryption Strict Privacy Compliance

Can I switch plans or add family members later?

Yes! You can easily upgrade from Individual to Priority Concierge or Family Package at any stage prior to final submission. The price difference is prorated without any penalty or administrative fees.

Flexible Plan Switching Prorated Difference

What if government authorities ask for revisions?

Your dedicated agent will handle all administrative correspondence. In the rare event that an issuing authority requests updated photos, revised notarization, or additional evidence, we guide you step-by-step and resubmit your application without charging extra service fees.

Free Application Resubmission Dedicated Agent Support

Do you offer doorstep document pickup & delivery?

Yes! Doorstep document pickup and physical dispatch delivery are included in our Priority Concierge and Family Packages across top metropolitan areas. For standard packages, secure courier tracking and digital submission links are provided.

Home Pickup (Select Cities) Tracked Dispatch
